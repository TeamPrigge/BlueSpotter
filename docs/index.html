Method

A segmentation model built for one nucleus

Bluespotter starts from Cellpose-SAM and fine-tunes it on more than 1,000 manually labelled LC segmentations, so it recognizes the shape, staining, and density of noradrenergic neurons specifically — not a general-purpose cell model asked to guess. Full methods and validation are described in our bioRxiv preprint.

—Fine-tuned specifically for LC neurons across staining protocols
—Reviewed against expert manual annotations before release
—Outputs both a visual mask overlay and structured per-cell measurements

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which image formats are supported?

Bluespotter accepts TIFF, PNG, JPEG, ND2, and CZI files — most formats exported directly from confocal, widefield, and slide-scanning microscopes.

How accurate is the segmentation?

The model is validated against expert manual annotations before release; accuracy figures and comparisons are reported in the preprint. Always visually review the overlay before quantifying.

Can I use this for other brainstem nuclei?

It is fine-tuned specifically for the locus coeruleus. Other nuclei may work but are out of the validated scope — treat results as exploratory.

Is my image data kept private?

Images are processed only to generate your results and are not shared. See the privacy note for details.

Do I need a GPU, and can I batch process images?

No local GPU is needed — inference runs on the hosted service (CPU by default, with an optional GPU tier for speed). Batch mode processes multiple images through the same endpoint.
